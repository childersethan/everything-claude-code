Privacy policy

Your privacy

True Flow Pump & Well Services ("True Flow", "we") runs trueflowpumpandwell.com. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Effective October 11, 2026.

What we collect

When you send a service request: your name, phone number, the property address or town if you give it, the kind of problem you pick, and anything you write in the message box. We only use this to contact you about your job and to keep a record of the work.

When you visit: like any website, our host keeps basic technical logs (such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to deliver the site and keep it secure. The site sets no cookies and runs no advertising scripts.

Visit counts: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they view. It sets no cookies, doesn't follow you to other sites and doesn't identify you. Cloudflare's privacy policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

When you call, text or email us: we receive whatever you send, the same as any phone call or email.

Who handles it

  • Netlify hosts the website, receives form submissions and keeps them in our account. Netlify checks submissions for spam using its spam-filtering providers.
  • Google delivers the email notification for each request to our Gmail inbox, and serves the fonts the site uses (your browser contacts Google's font servers to load them).
  • Cloudflare counts visits. Your browser sends it the page you viewed and basic device details, and Cloudflare sees your IP address when it does.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else for marketing. We may disclose it if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep service requests and job records for as long as we need them to serve you and for normal business records, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email trueflow2026@gmail.com or call or text (505) 793-5386. We won't send you marketing texts or emails unless you ask for them.

Children

This site is for people arranging water well and pump work. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date above.

Common questions

Before you call

My water just stopped. What should I check first?

Check the breaker for the well pump and look at the pressure gauge near your tank. If the breaker tripped twice or the gauge reads near zero, leave it off and call us. Running a struggling pump can burn it out.

Why does my pump turn on and off every few seconds?

That's short cycling, and it's usually a waterlogged pressure tank or a bad pre-charge. It wears out pumps fast, so it's worth fixing soon. Often it's a tank repair, not a new pump.

How long do well pumps last?

A submersible pump usually runs 8 to 15 years, depending on water quality, sand, and how often it cycles. A properly sized tank is the cheapest way to make a pump last longer.

Do you give estimates before starting?

Yes. We diagnose first, then give you the price and your options. Nothing gets pulled or replaced until you say go.

Do you do new well pump installs and upgrades?

Yes, including constant-pressure systems that keep steady pressure when two faucets run at once. We'll size the pump to your well's depth and yield.

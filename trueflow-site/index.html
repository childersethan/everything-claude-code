Questions

Good to know

Do you really answer at night and on weekends?

Yes. Water doesn't wait for business hours, and neither do we. Call or text any hour, any day.

What does it cost?

Every job starts with a free estimate. We look at your setup, tell you what's wrong and what it takes to fix, and you decide before any work starts.

How far do you travel?

About miles around Hoxie, Kansas, including Colby, Oakley, Hill City, Norton, Oberlin, Goodland, WaKeeney and Hays. Further out, call and ask.

What do you work on?

New water wells, house and irrigation wells, submersible pumps, pressure tanks, windmills, solar well systems, stock tanks and floats, trenching, water lines and leak repair.

My water just stopped. What should I check first?

Check the well breaker and whether the pressure tank gauge reads zero. If the breaker tripped twice, leave it off and call. Our symptom checker walks through the likely causes.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, True Flow Pump & Well is licensed and insured.
